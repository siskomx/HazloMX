Start by describing what you want to create, recreate or modify. If you do not know the measurements, first prepare an inspection/conversion copy with **Añadir un círculo a la copia** unchecked. Choose an editable output such as DXF. Confirm the original drawing's units; CADCraft rejects a declared unit mismatch. A unitless source needs a confirmed interpretation. Select the original, review the captured entity counts and operation, then approve. Open the conversion report to see the native result and any warnings. Files stay separate from the original.

For a circle, confirm where its center belongs relative to the drawing origin and its full width (diameter). The form explains horizontal and vertical position; its fields start empty.
