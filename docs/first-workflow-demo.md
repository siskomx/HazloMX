1. Open Hazlo Mexa and choose **Crear en carpeta…** in a new local project folder.
2. Expand **Ya sé las medidas / ajustar propuesta**. Choose millimeters and enter largo 10, ancho 20, alto 30.
3. Choose **Preparar propuesta**. Review the proportional preview and the 10 × 20 × 30 mm operation.
4. Switch to inches to see the same physical dimensions in the English/imperial system. Editing a value disables approval until **Actualizar propuesta** saves it.
5. If FreeCAD is unavailable, expand **FreeCAD 1.1.4** and select the package's **FreeCADCmd.exe**. Choose **Aprobar y generar** only after reviewing the dimensions.
6. Wait for separate generation, verification and publication results. Choose **Abrir FCStd** to inspect/edit the native box in FreeCAD, or **Abrir STEP** for the derivative. STEP may need isometric view and Fit All.
7. Close Hazlo Mexa, restart it and choose **Abrir proyecto…**. The saved result remains in history; reopening does not regenerate it.
8. Prepare another proposal and choose **Cancelar** to deny it. Prior results stay available. Cancellation that loses the publication race preserves the completed output.
