# Relative PCB movements through the built-in chat guide

Previously the built-in PCB guide always returned a fixed request for absolute coordinates. After opening and selecting an inspected footprint, a person can now say "muevelo 2 mm a la derecha" or "move it 0.1 inches left". Missing distances ask a direction-bound question; a later explicit distance with unit fills the draft. Unknown/uncertain answers leave the fields unchanged. The person selects the footprint by its measured label rather than remembering its reference or origin coordinates.

Supported directions are right/left/up/down in the board plane, with right increasing saved X and down increasing saved Y. KiCad's [developer coordinate documentation](https://dev-docs.kicad.org/en/import-formats/pads/index.html) confirms its Y-down coordinate system. The reply explains the convention and exact measured start/final position. The calculation always starts from the inspected position, rather than accumulating previous draft moves or assuming a mirrored/rotated live editor view. It preserves the other measured coordinate, layer and orientation. Broader placement intent uses the connected agents.

Distances accept supported explicit metric/imperial spellings and mils, decimal dot/comma and positive values. The guide rejects ambiguous/multiple instructions, uncertainty, missing units, negative/fraction/exponent/grouped inputs, zero/no-op/sub-nanometer moves, nonfinite positions and final coordinates outside the existing +/-10000 mm limit. It retains the backend's nanometer precision rather than rounding an unsupported requested move into a different one. Tiny arithmetic noise is normalized only after that precision check. Main and guide share the PCB unit factors.
